Every artist who has stepped aboard has left something behind — not an object, not a work hung on a wall, but a presence. A memory in the wood of the deck. A conversation that changed the light of a particular morning. A show that existed once, fully, and then the tide carried us forward.

This is where we keep them. Not as archive in the cold institutional sense — but as a living constellation. Port by port. Face by face. Story by story.

Find them on the map. Find them in the grid below. Every one of them became part of the telling.

This is an invitation before it is an application.

There is a kind of making that requires distance from the familiar. Stepping away from the studio, the city and the known. Moving toward something open, uncertain, alive and as open as the sea and horizon.

Portfolio invites artists to live, create and share work aboard our sailing vessel as we journey through the Mediterranean — port by port, community by community, tide by tide.

The Residency

What a Residency Looks Like

You will live aboard Portfolio as a full crew member, sailing between ports while developing work in direct response to the vessel, the sea, and the communities we encounter along the way.

Days at sea are yours. We ask only that you engage with the journey — the light, the movement, the people we meet — and that you bring something to share with the public upon arrival into port. Not a finished work necessarily, but an honest one. Something made from the crossing.

The length of your residency is something we determine together. Tell us what you need. We are listening.

The passage is the studio. The arrival is the gift.

Life Aboard

The Horizon Is Not.

🛏

Your Cabin

A private cabin for the duration of your residency. Shared communal spaces include the saloon, galley, and open deck — all available as studio and exhibition space.

🔧

Materials

Artists are encouraged to work with found, natural, or locally sourced materials that respond to the places we visit. We cannot support large scale production. We can support large scale thinking.

🌍

Community

Portfolio opens its decks at every port. As a resident artist you are invited to engage directly with the communities we encounter — to run conversations, lead workshops, share your process.

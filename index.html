Portfolio is a sailboat founded on an idea:

That there is a kind of making that requires distance from the familiar.

Portfolio is a floating art gallery sailing port to port across the Mediterranean, crewed by artists, open to strangers, guided by wind. At each harbor we drop anchor, open our doors, and invite the local art community aboard to gather, show work, and experience something that only this place, at this moment, could have made.

Portfolio is an artist collective, a residency and a traveling exhibition space. It is also something harder to name - a threshold, a chorus, a small act of rebellion in what becomes possible when creative people find each other at the water's edge.

Each show exists once, then the tide carries us forward.

The sea is our anchor. The horizon, our reference point. We move at the speed of the wind.
